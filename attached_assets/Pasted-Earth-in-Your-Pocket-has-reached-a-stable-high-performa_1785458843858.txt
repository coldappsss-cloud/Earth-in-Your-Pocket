Earth in Your Pocket has reached a stable, high-performance state. The app currently runs smoothly at approximately 60 FPS with an interactive 3D globe, realistic Earth textures, country borders, search, country highlighting, and detailed country information.

The objective of this update is NOT to redesign the application.

The objective is to make it feel like a premium, flagship mobile application while preserving the current architecture, smooth performance, and existing functionality.

====================================================
PERFORMANCE (Highest Priority)
====================================================

• Maintain the current smooth 60 FPS performance.
• Do not introduce frame drops.
• Do not reduce rendering quality.
• Do not remove any existing features.
• Preserve all existing touch gestures.
